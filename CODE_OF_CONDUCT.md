# Code of Conduct

CogCMS welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities and levels of experience. These rules apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces, including interactions with maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work respectfully. Give specific, useful feedback and leave room for questions and mistakes.
- Respect a person's identity, boundaries and time. Disagreement about a proposal is not permission to attack its author.
- Keep contributions relevant and protect private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, impersonation, persistent disruption and publishing another person's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

**Release preparation: maintainers must confirm a private conduct-reporting contact before publication.** Do not post sensitive allegations or personal details in a public issue. For abuse on GitHub, you can also use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request a change in behavior, restrict participation or exclude a participant. Decisions should consider severity, repeated behavior and the safety of those affected. Share report details only with people needed to assess and address the incident; do not promise absolute confidentiality. A participant may request reconsideration through the private reporting contact, supplying relevant context without continuing the disputed conduct.
